About

GW2 TOP 10 Crafting is a fan-made tool that helps Guild Wars 2 players identify the most profitable crafting opportunities across runes, sigils, relics and bags.

How it works

The tool fetches real-time prices from the official Guild Wars 2 Trading Post API and combines them with sales volume data from DataWars2 to calculate the top 10 most profitable items per category.
