GW2 TOP 10 Crafting

Unofficial tool for Guild Wars 2 players · Prices and sales data updated in real-time

Instructions:

  1. Enter the quantities you want to craft in Crafting column. Making sure not to exceed the quantities listed in the "Suggested Sales" column.
  2. Once you've finished entering the desired quantities, go to the shopping list to see the items and their quantities to purchase.
Note: Remember that some recipes require items linked to your account, so you must already have those materials in your inventory.
